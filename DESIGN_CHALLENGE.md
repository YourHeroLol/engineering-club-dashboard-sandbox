# Engineering Club Dashboard Design Challenge

## Goal

Design a member and officer portal that helps people find useful information
quickly on both phones and computers.

You are not expected to preserve the starter's appearance. The starter exists to
provide working navigation, role simulation, data shapes, and safety boundaries.

## Required experiences

### Registered member

- View upcoming meetings and events
- View their own fictional profile
- Cannot open the officer overview or roster

### Officer

- Use the member portal
- View the officer overview
- Search and filter the fictional roster by membership year
- View name, nickname, pronouns, major, portal email, volunteer interests, and
  raffle tickets
- Cannot receive phone numbers

### Executive officer

- Has officer access
- Can also receive fictional phone numbers
- Represents the President, Vice President, and Secretary in production

## Design freedom

You may change:

- Navigation pattern
- Layout and information hierarchy
- Color and typography
- Dashboard cards and charts
- Roster presentation
- Profile presentation
- Mobile behavior
- Empty, loading, and error states

You may add mock features such as announcements or project opportunities, but
label them clearly and use fictional data.

## Suggested voting rubric

| Category | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Usability and clarity | 30% |
| Accessibility and mobile experience | 25% |
| Visual design | 20% |
| Useful officer workflow ideas | 15% |
| Code organization and explanation | 10% |

The winning design will still receive a privacy, security, accessibility, and
maintainability review before any part is moved into production.
